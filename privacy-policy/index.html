Privacy Policy for Stuffer

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Stuffer ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mobile application designed to help users organize and remember where they keep their belongings.

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Stuffer handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

Stuffer does not require you to create an account or provide personal information.

The app does not collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Account credentials
  • Payment information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Analytics data

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Stuffer allows you to create places and items to help organize your belongings.

Information you enter into the app, such as:

  • Place names
  • Item names
  • Item descriptions
  • Item dates
  • Place icons and colors
  • Photos selected from your device's gallery
  • App language preference
  • Dark-mode preference

is stored locally on your device.

This information is not transmitted to our servers because Stuffer does not currently use a cloud-storage or synchronization service.

3. Photos

Stuffer may allow you to select photos from your device's gallery and associate them with items.

Stuffer does not upload these photos to our servers or share them with third parties.

Photos remain stored locally on your device as part of the app's data.

Stuffer does not use the camera.

4. Internet and Data Transmission

The current version of Stuffer does not require an internet connection for its core functionality.

Stuffer does not transmit your stored places, items, descriptions, or photos to a remote server.

5. Third-Party Services

The current version of Stuffer does not use third-party analytics, advertising, authentication, cloud-backup, or tracking services.

6. Advertising

The current version of Stuffer does not display advertisements.

If advertising is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain how advertising-related information is handled.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Because Stuffer does not currently collect personal information or transmit your app data to our servers, there is no user database maintained by us containing your places, items, or photos.

8. Data Security

Your Stuffer data is stored locally on your device. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and protecting access to it.

Because Stuffer does not currently provide cloud backup, deleting the application or losing your device may result in the loss of locally stored app data.

9. Children's Privacy

Stuffer does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

Because the app does not require an account or collect personal information, no personal information is knowingly collected from children through the app.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application changes or when additional features or services are introduced.

Any updated Privacy Policy will be made available through the appropriate application or website.
